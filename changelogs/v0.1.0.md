## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- **Hearth Trophy Attunement**: Mount creature trophies on item stands near comfort sources and attune (`Shift + E`) to channel passive combat and survival rites while Rested.
- **Configurable 24×4 Comfort Progression**: Power scales across Comfort levels 1–24 and unlocks up to 4 trophy slots using configurable Reservoir, Uniform, or Custom progression models.
- **Day-1 Meadows Item Stands**: Added an early-game crafting recipe for vertical and horizontal item stands (4 Wood + 1 Leather Scraps at a Workbench), enabling early trophy attunement.
- **55 Creature Rites & 16 Set Synergies**: Unique passive rites for every native Valheim creature trophy and thematic multi-trophy set bonuses scaled through VikingLib's stat engine.
- **Dynamic Slot Feedback**: Clear in-game hover text indicating active anchor, secondary slots, and current comfort scaling.
