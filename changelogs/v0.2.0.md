## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-25

### Added
- **Configurable 24×4 Comfort Progression**: Power scales across Comfort levels 1–24 and unlocks up to 4 trophy slots using configurable Reservoir, Uniform, or Custom progression models.
- **Day-1 Meadows Item Stands**: Added an early-game crafting recipe for vertical and horizontal item stands (4 Wood + 1 Leather Scraps at a Workbench), enabling early trophy attunement.
- **Dynamic Slot Hover Feedback**: Clear in-game hover text indicating active anchor, secondary slots, and current comfort scaling.

### Changed
- **55-Creature Rite & Set Synergy Rebalance**: Recalibrated rite power and set synergy bonuses across all biome trophies to align with canonical VikingLib stat tokens.
